Frequently asked questions

Everything regulated teams ask before adopting Conductor.

How does Conductor keep spend under control?

You set a hard budget cap. Conductor enforces it mathematically — agents cannot initiate an action that would exceed the limit, and every dollar is attributed to an approved sprint.

What exactly requires human approval?

Any risky action — spending money, running database migrations, and deploying — passes through an approval gate. You green-light work sprint by sprint before anything ships.

Is the audit trail exportable?

Yes. Conductor records who approved what and when in an immutable, exportable log — built for compliance review and regulated environments.

How is generated software verified?

Deliverables are tested in a real browser before delivery, and agents self-repair failures automatically. You only review output that already passes checks.

Do I need to change my existing stack?

No. Conductor layers governance on top of autonomous agents — you keep your tools and get approval gates, caps, and audit trails around them.
